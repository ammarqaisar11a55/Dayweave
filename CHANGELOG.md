# Changelog

## 0.1.0

The first version.

### Added

- Tasks with priorities, statuses, categories, tags, nested subtasks and estimates
- Date-only and exact deadlines, with reminders and an overdue view
- Repeating tasks with daily, weekly, monthly, yearly and custom rules
- Projects with progress and their own task views
- Habits to build, quit, measure or check off, with streaks, history and a heatmap
- A daily overview with a woven picture of the day's progress
- A calendar with month, week and day views, and drag to reschedule
- Analytics for tasks, habits, focus time and projects
- Focus sessions with breaks and history
- Desktop notifications with Complete and Snooze buttons, and a notification center
- Background reminders from the tray, with an option to start at login
- Search, a command palette and keyboard shortcuts
- Light, dark and system themes
- Full backups, JSON and CSV export, and validated restore with a preview
- Snap, `.deb` and AppImage packages
